Privacy Policy

📅 Last Updated: January 13, 2026

🌟 Introduction

List-e ("App") is a mobile application that makes it easy for families to share shopping lists. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data the app collects and how it is used.

📊 Data We Collect

1. Data Provided by You

  • Shopping List Data: Product names and quantities you add
  • Room Codes: 6-digit codes you create for sharing

2. Automatically Collected Data

  • Usage Statistics: Which products are frequently added (anonymous)
  • Device Information: Basic device info for crash reports

❌ Data We Do Not Collect

  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Name / Identity information
  • Location data
  • Camera / Photos
  • Contacts list

🎯 How We Use Data

Collected data is used solely for the following purposes:

  • Synchronizing your shopping lists
  • Suggesting frequently purchased products
  • Improving app performance

🔒 Data Storage

Your data is stored on Google Firebase secure servers:

  • Encrypted connection (SSL/TLS)
  • Industry-standard security
  • Only people who know the room code can access your data

🤝 Data Sharing

Your data is never shared with or sold to third parties. Data is only:

  • Shared with family members using the same room code
  • Stored on Firebase infrastructure (subject to Google's privacy policy)

👶 Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. Parental supervision is recommended.

🗑️ Deleting Your Data

You can delete your data at any time:

  • Delete list items individually
  • Create a new room by changing your room code
  • For complete data deletion: omer_nacar@hotmail.com

🔄 Policy Changes

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Significant changes will be announced within the app.
